## About

Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering graduate (2026) focused on **embedded firmware development and low-level systems**.

I enjoy understanding how software interacts with hardware — from microcontroller peripherals and communication protocols to registers, timing, interrupts, and real hardware validation. My approach is to understand the underlying hardware and protocol first, then translate that understanding into simple, reliable firmware.

Alongside embedded systems, I have a strong interest in **VLSI and digital design**, with hands-on experience taking RTL through a complete open-source RTL-to-GDS flow targeting Sky130.

I also have multiple IEEE publications spanning embedded systems, robotics, and digital design.
